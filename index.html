Moodle Guide for PhD Candidates

A step-by-step walkthrough covering course access, research milestone submissions, quizzes, and secure exit procedures.

1. Logging In & Accessing Courses

  1. Open your browser and navigate to the university Moodle portal.
  2. Log in using your institutional credentials or the Single Sign-On (SSO) button.
  3. On your Dashboard, locate the Course Overview block.
  4. Filter by In Progress to locate your dissertation shells, research colloquiums, or seminar modules.
  5. Click on the course title or card to enter.
🔗 Visual Documentation: MoodleDocs: Course Overview Interface

2. Submitting an Assignment (Chapters & Milestones)

  1. Click on the assignment link on your course page (marked with a clipboard icon).
  2. Review the Due date, file size limits, and accepted formats (e.g., PDF, DOCX).
  3. Click the Add submission button.
  4. Drag and drop your file into the submission box or click Add... to browse your files.
  5. Tick the academic integrity statement checkbox if prompted.
  6. Click Save changes.
  7. Confirm the status shows Submitted for grading in green. If it says Draft (not submitted), click Submit assignment and confirm.
🔗 Visual Documentation: MoodleDocs: Assignment Submission Walkthrough

3. Attempting Quizzes & Comprehensive Assessments

  1. Click on the quiz link from the course outline.
  2. Review the time limit, allowed attempts, and instructions.
  3. Click Attempt quiz now, then click Start attempt.
  4. Answer the questions. Use the Quiz Navigation block on the side to verify all questions are completed.
  5. Click Finish attempt... at the bottom of the page.
  6. Verify all questions indicate Answer saved on the summary page.
  7. Click Submit all and finish, and confirm in the dialog prompt.
🔗 Visual Documentation: MoodleDocs: Quiz Navigation & Summary

4. Viewing Grades & Supervisor Feedback

  1. Return to the assignment link and scroll down to the Feedback section.
  2. Review supervisor comments, download annotated feedback files, or inspect grading rubrics.
  3. To view an overview of all course marks, click Grades in the top course menu.
🔗 Visual Documentation: MoodleDocs: Student Gradebook View

5. Logging Out Securely

  1. Click your profile picture or initials in the top-right corner of the page.
  2. Select Log out from the dropdown menu.
  3. Close your browser tabs completely, especially when working on shared research lab terminals.
